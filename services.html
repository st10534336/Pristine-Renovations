Services We Offer Our Clients

Project Planning

Drawing up plans and getting the necessary approval from your local council or municipality

We will work together to plan the design, layout, materials, permits, timeline and most importantly the budget

Room Renovations

We can help you to completely overhaul your Kitchens, bathrooms and living areas

Carpet can be removed to be replaced with wooden floors and tiles, Wooden kitchen counter can be replaced with luxury marble etc.

Home Extensions

We can help with extending to your homes adding to its depth and creating more living space.

We can build extra rooms, enclose patios etc, adding swimming pools

Structural Alterations

Your buildings walls will be broken down to open up the space more.

An example would be removing the interior wall between your living room an kitchen to create an open plan

Plumbing and Electrical Work

All rewiring, pipe upgrades and light fixtures will be handled by our experts

You will not be left stranded in a lovely renovated home that lacks working electricity and no flowing water in your faucets

This is not a standalone service but one we do if necessary depending on the scale of the renovation

Painting and Waterproofing

A new coat and be added to the interiors and exteriors of your property, bringing new life to them

How do we decide on the Price of the Renovation?

1.We will have an in person or online consultation

2.Should the online consultation go well, we will meet at the job site to take measurements, inspect the building and you'll tell us your layout ideas

3.Materials will be selected depending on what is needed and also what you want

4.Based on the selected layout and materials, we will give you a solid cost breakdown that fits your budget while keeping your dream renovation possible

5.Once we agree on the cost we will draw up a timeline for how long the renovation will potentially take.

6.Our licensing team will get the necessary paperwork in order to begin the construction

(The time between the first consultation to beginning with construction work is usually about 2-4 weeks)

7.We will keep in contact with you from the beginning of the project to the end

8.After the completion of the project, we will walk through your newly renovated space and ensure that everything is to the tea

We also provide a 1 year worksmanship gaurantee
